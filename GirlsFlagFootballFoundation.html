Who We Are

Built for this gap.
Built for these girls.

Girls' flag football is one of the fastest-growing youth sports in Northern California — and one of the most underfunded. While the NFL and USA Football have invested heavily in growing the game, the financial infrastructure to support individual athletes lagged years behind.

The Girls Flag Football Foundation was founded in 2026 by Silicon Valley executives who believe every girl deserves the opportunity to experience the confidence, leadership, teamwork, and life skills that athletics can provide — regardless of her family's financial circumstances.

We are headquartered in Campbell, Northern California, and serve athletes from Fresno County north to the Oregon border.

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit governed by a volunteer board of directors. Every dollar raised goes toward direct athlete assistance, operating costs, or building the infrastructure that sustains this work long-term.

Build With Us

Girls Flag Football Foundation doesn't just
fund athletes.

We help clubs, schools, brands, and community leaders build the girls flag football ecosystem across Northern California. If you're invested in growing this sport, we want to build with you.

We build the
ecosystem.

The Girls Flag Football Foundation is more than a scholarship program. We are building the connective tissue of girls' flag football in NorCal — linking clubs, schools, brands, equipment suppliers, and community organizations into a coordinated network that makes the sport more accessible for every girl in the region.

Partner Types

Six ways to build with us.

🏈
Club Partners

Flag football clubs in NorCal who partner with GFF gain a direct pipeline to scholarship-supported athletes who strengthen their rosters — at no cost to the club.

  • Priority access to scholarship athlete referrals
  • Co-branded athlete support communications
  • Simple eligibility verification process
  • Joint storytelling and social recognition
  • Recognition as a Girls Flag Football Foundation partner club
🏫
School Partners

Middle and high schools building flag football programs can partner with GFF to connect student-athletes to funding, gear, and coaching development resources.

  • Gear and equipment support for school teams
  • Coaching certification pathways for staff
  • Scholarship referrals for qualifying athletes
  • Co-branded program recognition
  • Access to tournament and travel assistance
👑
Brand Partners

Brands that share our values of equity, athletic access, and women's leadership can partner beyond standard sponsorship — co-creating campaigns, content, and community moments.

  • Co-branded content and campaign opportunities
  • Athlete ambassador introductions
  • Event presence at GFF tournaments
  • Impact storytelling for brand channels
  • Custom partnership structures available
🗻
Equipment Partners

Sporting goods brands and equipment manufacturers can supply gear at cost or through in-kind donation, directly equipping GFF-funded athletes across NorCal.

  • In-kind donation acknowledgment and receipting
  • Logo placement on gear provided to athletes
  • Impact reporting (units provided, athletes served)
  • Feature in annual impact report
  • Preferred vendor status for GFF gear programs
🏆
Tournament Partners

Tournament organizers who partner with GFF provide priority access and travel support to GFF-funded athletes, expanding competitive opportunity across NorCal.

  • Co-promotion to GFF athlete and donor audience
  • GFF banner and recognition at events
  • Featured in GFF athlete communications
  • Priority registration access for GFF athletes
  • Joint storytelling post-event
🏠
Community Partners

Community organizations, civic leaders, and local institutions can partner with GFF to expand our reach and connect underserved athletes to the program.

  • Co-referral program for qualifying athletes
  • Joint outreach and community events
  • Recognition as a GFF Community Partner
  • Shared storytelling and impact reporting
  • Invitation to annual partner summit
